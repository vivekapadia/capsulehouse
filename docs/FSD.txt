This plan outlines the digital strategy to establish a high-end online presence for your capsule house manufacturing business, moving from a local development environment to a robust in-house hosting setup.
1. Website Development Plan
To offer a "Basic to Luxury" user experience, the website will act as a virtual showroom.
Product Tiering: Create dedicated landing pages for different lines:
Basic: Focus on affordability, utility, and quick setup (Apple Cabins).
Luxury: Focus on smart tech, panoramic views, and premium finishes (Space Capsules).
3D Configurator: An interactive tool where users can select "Basic" or "Luxury" finishes, seeing real-time price updates in INR.
Virtual Tours: Integration of 360° videos or Matterport tours to give a "luxury" sense of space.
Lead Generation: High-intent forms for "Request a Quote" and "Download Technical Specifications."
2. Technical Stack Implementation
Your chosen stack is high-performance and scalable for a Pan-India rollout.
Frontend (Next.js):
SSR/SSG: Use Static Site Generation for fast-loading product pages to improve SEO.
UI Library: Use Tailwind CSS or Framer Motion for "luxury" animations and smooth transitions.
Backend (Go/Golang):
Speed: Go’s concurrency handles high traffic during marketing campaigns effortlessly.
API: RESTful or GraphQL API to serve product data and handle lead captures.
Database & ORM (Postgres + GORM):
Postgres (Dockerized): Ensures a consistent environment from your local machine to the in-house server.
GORM: Simplifies complex queries for filtering houses by size (sq. ft./Gaj) and price tier.
3. Hosting & Infrastructure (Local to In-House)
Since your objective is in-house hosting, the transition follows these steps:
Phase 1: Local Development: Running the entire stack (Go, Postgres, Next.js) via Docker Compose on local workstations.
Phase 2: In-House Server Setup:
Deploy a dedicated Linux server (e.g., Ubuntu Server).
Reverse Proxy: Use Nginx or Traefik to handle SSL certificates (HTTPS) and route traffic to your Docker containers.
Phase 3: Public Access:
Set up a Static IP with your ISP.
Configure Port Forwarding and Firewall (UFW) to safely expose the Next.js frontend to the internet.
4. Manufacturing & Operations Integration
The website should reflect your manufacturing strengths:
Inventory Tracking: Use the Go backend to sync with your factory’s production schedule.
Transparency: A "Track My Build" feature for customers to see their capsule house moving through the manufacturing stages.
Logistics Module: A calculator to estimate transportation costs from your hub to the customer's specific location in India.
5. Content Strategy (Referencing Hitechnest Style)
Portfolio: High-resolution galleries of completed projects across India (e.g., resorts in Goa vs. cottages in Manali).
Technical Specs: Clear tables showing dimensions in Sq. Ft. and Gaj, material grades, and earthquake/wind resistance ratings.
Trust Signals: Prominently display ISO certifications and "Make in India" credentials.
